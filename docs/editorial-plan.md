# What earns an episode

A real fight announcement, a result or a concrete news development about a Montana fighter. Amateur and professional coverage both belong here. No weekly quota and no generic gym profile without a news reason.

## Source desk

The maintained directory is [sources.json](../public/sources.json). It has 17 entries across promoters, venues, local reporting, gyms, records and leads that still need checking. The production research prompt reads this same directory.

Start with promoter announcements, venue pages and original local reporting. Use dated gym or fighter posts for amateur debuts and team changes. Confirm identity through official links. Social feeds that require sign in need manual review. Do not treat a search snippet or a copied social post as a retrieved original source.

Montana Sports and its local stations can carry the same story. That is one reporting source, not independent corroboration. Sherdog and Tapology help check records and results. Evidence from only those databases is now rejected.

Fusion's official link hub is useful for finding individual cards. Its main site currently exposes very little readable text. Mountain Force is regional, so every story still needs a Montana connection. KillEagle has documented Montana history but a current schedule was not verified. FightForce's old ticket page is not a current schedule. Montana MMA's old site points to its current domain, which could not be read during this check.

## Dates

Archive labels describe the period covered. Production and publication dates stay accurate. A retrospective can use later verified results, but must not pretend they were known before the bout. Reconstructed previews require evidence that was available at their coverage date. No fabricated release history.

## Sound

Start on the news. Brief show identification after the hook. Use contractions and concrete details. Avoid canned excitement, repeated caveats and explanations of the research process. Keep essential attribution in the script and put fuller limitations in the notes. AI voice disclosure stays on the page.
