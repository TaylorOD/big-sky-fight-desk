A NOTE FROM THE DESK

The whole local card matters.

Amateur debuts belong here alongside the pro main event. So do new matchups, results and real news about Montana fighters. No episode quota. No made up drama to fill the quiet weeks.

Fusion is a starting point, not the whole scene. We follow other promoters when there's a confirmed Montana connection. MMA, boxing, kickboxing and bare knuckle get their own labels.

This is independent coverage, with AI assisted scripts and an AI generated voice. Sources stay with every episode. Illustrated covers are artwork, not photos of actual fights. We are not affiliated with any promotion.
