Fusion Fight League is our starting point. We also follow confirmed Montana events from other promoters and Montana fighters competing farther from home.

Every episode starts with research. We separate announcements from results, label different combat sports clearly and leave unsupported claims out.

The scripts and voice are made with AI and reviewed before publication. Sources and the research date stay with each episode so you can check the reporting yourself.

Illustrated covers are original AI artwork. They do not depict actual fights. This show is not affiliated with any promotion.
